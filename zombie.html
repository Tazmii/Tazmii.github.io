Experiment 968: Nightlight Corpse

This is an original animation that combines 3D Blender animation with various post-processing and video editing effects to tell a short narrative. It tells a story of a mysterious company conducting experiments on an innocent man, turning him into a zombie-like monster.

The main animation is created in 3D. After the initial character design was drawn, I adapted it into 3D, using a toon shader to mimic 2D illustrations. The model was made with 2 seperate textures, which were able to be tranitioned between each other in the material. The model was then fully rigged an animated within the environment, which I also modeled and textured.

Once the main animation was completed, extensive work was done in AdobePremiere Pro. The final video features multiple slides made to look like classified documents of the company. They feature multiple illustrations and writing to set the stage and to wind down after. I also put great work to simulate the look of old found-footage, giving the whole video and unnerving feel.

Logo design was done for the video thumbnail and the in-universe company logo. This was done in Adobe Illustrator. The musical jingle and other sound design work was also done by me.
