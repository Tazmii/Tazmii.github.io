Quadrupedal Machine (Oli)

A quadrupedal robot I designed and modeled to have an intentionally strange appearance. Each leg is uniquely designed and are connected to the main body at different elevations. The head features several moving parts, such as 6 rotating video screens and an array of weaponry.
